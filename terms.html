2. What SanctumBoard is

SanctumBoard is a governance platform with an AI company secretary ("Pam") that drafts, prepares, chases and tracks — and a sealing mechanism by which a named human approves before anything becomes a record.

The seal is architectural, not a policy. Pam cannot create a record of a decision. Every consequential act waits for an approval carrying a person's name and the time they gave it. We could not change this by changing a setting, and neither can you — it is how the product is built.

SanctumBoard supports your governance. It does not provide legal advice, audit opinions, or compliance determinations, and it does not exercise judgement on your board's behalf. Your directors' duties remain entirely your directors'.

3. The free trial

New organisations may take one trial of 30 days. It includes the full product and 1,000 AI credits. There is no credit card, nothing to cancel, and nothing is charged at any point during or after it.

  • One trial per organisation. We identify an organisation by its email domain, so several directors at the same organisation share one trial.
  • Scheduled AI work — daily briefings and similar — is off during a trial. You can ask Pam anything you like; she simply does not run unattended.
  • When the trial ends, the board becomes read-only.

Nothing is deleted when a trial ends. Every paper, minute, decision and register stays exactly where you left it and stays readable. What stops is adding anything new. If you subscribe later — a week later or a year later — you carry on from where you stopped.

We may decline or limit trials, including where an organisation has already had one. We will always tell you why, and you are welcome to contact us.

4. Subscriptions and payment

Every plan includes unlimited board members. You are employing a secretary, not licensing seats, and adding a director never changes your price.

Payments are processed by Stripe. We do not see or store your card details. If a payment fails we will tell you and give you a reasonable opportunity to fix it before anything is suspended.

We may change prices with 30 days' notice. A change never affects a term you have already paid for: an annual subscriber keeps the price they paid until their renewal date.

5. AI credits

Each plan includes a monthly allowance of AI credits. Credits are consumed when Pam does work — answering a question, drafting a paper, compiling a pack.

  • Included monthly credits expire 90 days after they are granted. Credits you purchase as a pack do not expire.
  • The oldest credits are always spent first, so an expiring monthly grant is used before a pack you paid for.
  • When credits run out, Pam stops until your next renewal or until you buy more. Everything else in the product keeps working.

You are never charged for going over. There is no overage billing and no automatic top-up. Running out is a stop, not a bill — and we tell you when you reach 90% of your allowance so it is never a surprise.

Credits have no cash value, are not refundable, and cannot be transferred between organisations.

Annual subscriptions

An annual subscription is a commitment for the year, in exchange for a 10% discount on the monthly price. If you cancel before the year is up:

8. Your records

Your board's content is yours. Papers, minutes, decisions, registers and everything else you put into SanctumBoard remain your property. We claim no ownership of them and no right to use them beyond operating the service for you.

We do not use your board's content to train AI models. It is not shared with other customers, and it is not sold to anybody, ever.

Board content is stored and processed in Australia, including AI inference. How we handle personal information, and the limited circumstances in which anything crosses a border, is set out in our Privacy Policy.

Two records cannot be edited or deleted, by us or by you, and you should know this before you rely on the product:

  • The audit log and decision ledger are append-only. A correction is made by adding an entry, never by altering one. This is the property that makes a Governance Passport worth anything — a record that could be quietly changed proves nothing.
  • The credit ledger is a financial record and is retained accordingly.

Where a person exercises a right to erasure, we revoke their access and remove their personal details from everywhere we can, while the fact that a named person approved a decision on a date remains — because that is the board's record of its own governance, not ours to erase.

11. What AI does and does not do

Pam drafts and prepares. She does not decide, and she cannot create a record without a named human approval.

  • AI output is a draft until a person seals it, and is labelled as such throughout the product.
  • Pam cites her sources, or says plainly that evidence is missing. She is built not to assert what she cannot support — but AI can still be wrong, and the seal is where you catch it.
  • Obligations, duty maps and Board Health scores are governance support. They are not legal advice and not a determination that you comply with anything.
  • A Governance Passport attests to process adherence and evidence integrity. It does not certify that your board is well-governed, and we make no such claim to any third party.
  • You can turn AI off entirely, function by function, and SanctumBoard remains a complete governance system.
