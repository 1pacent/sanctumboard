Security by Design

Your Data Stays
In Your Hands

Every architectural decision in SanctumBoard was made to ensure that sensitive board information never leaves your control. This is not a policy — it is the technical reality.

On-Premises AI

The language model (Gemma 3, running via Ollama) is installed directly on your server. There is no API call to OpenAI, Anthropic, or any external provider. Inference is 100% local.

Isolated Infrastructure

Each organisation runs in its own containerised stack — separate database, separate LLM context, separate network segment. A vulnerability in one tenant cannot cascade to another.

Role-Based Access

Directors, Secretaries and Chairs see only what their role authorises. Board papers, risk registers, and strategic documents are access-controlled at the row level — not just the page level.

Full Audit Trail

Every AI interaction, document access, vote cast and action recorded is timestamped and attributed to an individual director. You have a verifiable record for regulators, insurers, and legal review.

Board papers contain your most sensitive strategic and financial information. Sending them to a public AI API creates legal, regulatory, and reputational risk. SanctumBoard was built from the ground up to eliminate this risk — not manage it.

Industry-Specific Intelligence

Governance Support
Built for Your Sector

Generic AI does not understand aged care quality standards or AICD fiduciary obligations. SanctumBoard's AI is grounded in the regulatory and governance frameworks that govern your organisation.

Aged Care

The Aged Care Act 1997 and its 2024 reforms impose specific obligations on approved provider boards. SanctumBoard's AI understands the Aged Care Quality Standards, the role of the Aged Care Quality and Safety Commission, and the board's duty to ensure safe, person-centred care.

  • Aged Care Quality and Safety Commission (ACQSC) framework
  • Eight Aged Care Quality Standards compliance tracking
  • Serious Incident Response Scheme (SIRS) obligations
  • Aged Care (Transitional Provisions) Act reform readiness
  • Statement of Rights and person-centred governance

NFP & Charity Boards

Most Common

Not-for-profit boards operate under the ACNC Act, state-based associations legislation, and heightened public scrutiny. AI assistance that is tuned to mission-driven governance obligations — not corporate profit maximisation.

  • ACNC governance standards and reporting obligations
  • Duty of care and duty of diligence for NFP directors
  • Related-party transaction management
  • Charity registration and charitable purpose compliance
  • Grant acquittals and program governance oversight

Corporate & ASX

For private and publicly listed companies, SanctumBoard aligns to the AICD Code of Conduct for Directors, ASX Corporate Governance Principles and Recommendations, and Corporations Act 2001 obligations.

  • AICD Code of Conduct for Directors
  • ASX CGC Principles and Recommendations (4th edition)
  • Corporations Act 2001 — ss 180–184 director duties
  • Board committee oversight (Audit, Risk, Remuneration)
  • Modern Slavery Act reporting and supply chain governance

Aligned to AICD Governance Principles

The Australian Institute of Company Directors defines the gold standard for director conduct in Australia. SanctumBoard's AI is grounded in AICD frameworks — covering director duties, board composition, oversight of management, and accountability to stakeholders. Deployment

Up and Running in
Days, Not Months

SanctumBoard deploys on your existing server infrastructure. No cloud migration, no vendor lock-in, no per-seat SaaS pricing.

01

Provision Your Server

Any modern Linux server — on-premises or your preferred cloud provider. We support dedicated hardware, private cloud (AWS/Azure/GCP VPC), and co-located data centres. Minimum 16GB RAM for the LLM inference engine.

02

We Deploy & Configure

Our team installs the Docker stack — governance platform, private LLM, vector database, and reverse proxy with TLS. We load your organisation's governance documents and configure the AI's system prompt with your regulatory context.

03

Board Onboarding

Directors receive role-based access. A 90-minute onboarding session covers the board pack, AI assistant, and voting workflows. The Secretary gets a dedicated session on meeting management and paper authoring.
