The platform

One secretary. One seal. One source of proof.

P

Pam does the work

Agendas drafted, reports commissioned and chased, packs compiled and distributed, minutes assembled before the room empties, actions tracked, regulators watched. She remembers your board — what each director cares about, what was decided and why — and she works every day, not just before meetings.

You hold the seal

Pam proposes; a named human approves; then the record exists. Every consequential act — an agenda, a resolution, the minutes — waits for a seal with a name and a time on it. The board keeps its authority and gains a perfect account of exercising it.
